The Career Mode Promise

EA Sports rates every young footballer's potential. We followed 48 FIFA 15 wonderkids and 11,519 young players into real careers to see how often the game was right.

By Sahil Mohanty, Devarshi Mahajan and Sarthak Sonawane

Act 1

The promise

Every autumn, EA Sports rates the world's young footballers and gives each a potential: the rating the game says he can grow into. Millions of Career Mode players build squads around those numbers. In FIFA 15 (September 2014), 48 under-21s were rated 85 or higher: the game's wonderkids.

Over the next five editions EA handed out far more of them. By FIFA 20 there were 37.2 wonderkid ratings per 1,000 under-21s, 3.6× the FIFA 15 rate, while the average under-21's current ability rose only 1.7 points.

Act 2

The reckoning

We followed 11,519 young entrants from FIFA 15 to FIFA 18 through every later edition up to FC 24. Most promises weren't kept: among prospects rated 75+, 83% peaked below their potential, and every 10 points of promise delivered only 6.1 points of real rating.

Yet the promise still meant something. Only 11% of 85+ prospects reached their number in the game, but 68% built real Big-5 careers (3,000+ league minutes), against 4% of those rated under 70. And as the wonderkid ratings multiplied, fewer came true: 25% of FIFA 15 entrants reached their potential within four editions, 6% of FIFA 20's.

Act 3

Who the game misjudged

To compare groups fairly we measure each player against others given the same potential. By that yardstick EA over-promised most for the youngest: 17-year-olds got 15.1 points of headroom, nearly twice a 21-year-old's, and fell 3.5 points further short.

The big-club badge, a common fan suspicion, barely mattered: an elite club added about 1.1 potential points compared with a weak one, and those players lived up to it. Among positions, goalkeepers and strikers fell short most.

Act 4

What actually mattered

As a ranking, the game's potential worked about as well as the transfer market. Measured by AUC (the chance of ranking a player who made it above one who didn't, where 0.5 is a coin flip), EA potential scored 0.71 and Transfermarkt market value at the same moment 0.72. The number overstated the size of the gap between players, but the order was largely right.

Act 5

Where they are now

Of 2,488 prospects rated potential 75+, 17% play in a Big-5 league today. H. Kane outgrew a modest promise; R. Živković never came close to his; M. Depay now plays for Sport Club Corinthians Paulista.

Method. Cohort: each player's first FIFA 15–18 edition at age 21 or under (11,519 players). Peak later rating: best overall in any later edition up to FC 24. Made it: 3,000+ Big-5 league minutes after entry (Transfermarkt). Shortfall vs similar prospects: typical peak for a given potential minus the player's actual peak.
